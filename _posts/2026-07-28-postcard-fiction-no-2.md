You know, I actually wrote this one a while ago, but I just got around to posting it. I go through hyperfocus periods where I'm obsessed with updating my website and then I forget it exists completely. Since last time, I've kind of fallen off the horse with writing again, but as I always say, the pen (or keyboard in my case) is always patiently waiting. It doesn't judge you for your absence, it's just happy to see you again. Maybe one day if I keep writing 100 words one little session at a time I'll finally have written that novel I've always wanted to write. Hopefully, you'll hear from me sooner than the last one...

---

The vent fan whirs overhead as a wisp of smoke rises from the sizzling pan. The savory scent of seared trout trails into the air, so enticing it causes the smoke alarm to scream with desire. Like a bird taking flight, the yellow towel swoops through the air. The cry is silenced, only to be met with the sweet melody of the rice cooker, erupting with steam. As two glistening white lumps are scooped into bowls, the microwave beeps with glee. Green broccoli hedges frame the rice lawn with the trout placed as a shining pink flamingo. The kitchen sighs.
